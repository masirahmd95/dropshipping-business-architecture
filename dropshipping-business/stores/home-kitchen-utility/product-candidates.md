# Product Candidates: Home/Kitchen Utility

## Purpose

This file tracks product ideas before they are approved for the home/kitchen utility store.

Definition:
- Product candidate: a product idea being researched before it becomes a live store product.

## Decision Status Definitions

- Candidate: worth researching.
- Test: ready for sample order or small market test.
- Approved: passed demand, supplier, margin, compliance, and visual checks.
- Blocked: should not be sold.

## Store-Specific Approval Rules

- Do not approve without measured sample dimensions.
- Do not approve if supplier images hide scale.
- Do not approve food-contact claims without evidence.
- Do not approve if shipping damage risk is high and packaging is weak.

Definition:
- Scale: how large the product appears compared with a familiar object, hand, drawer, counter, or appliance.
