# Top 30 Sample Order Pack

## Purpose

This pack turns the top 30 beauty product candidates into an operating order list for supplier sourcing and sample testing.

Definitions:
- Sample order: buying a product before selling it so quality, packaging, delivery, and supplier reliability can be checked.
- Product file: the proof record for one product.
- Market eligibility: whether a product can be sold in a specific market, such as the United States or United Kingdom.
- Direct sale: selling the product through our own store and taking responsibility for customer service, refunds, and product claims.

Business meaning:
- The next practical step is not branding.
- The next practical step is supplier link collection, sample ordering, sample inspection, and product-file evidence.

## Ordering Rule

Order the top 30 samples, but inspect them in waves.

Recommended execution:
- Wave 1: products 1-15 in the operating sample plan.
- Wave 2: products 16-30 immediately after supplier links and budget are confirmed.

Business reason:
- Ordering all 30 is acceptable for a serious validation push.
- Inspecting in waves keeps evidence, photos, delivery notes, and defects organized.

## Inspection Requirements By Lane

Lane 1:
- Check quality, finish, size, material, packaging, and listing accuracy.
- Check supplier photos against real sample.
- Check US/UK shipping coverage.

Lane 2:
- Check all Lane 1 items.
- Check skin or eye-adjacent material.
- Check hygiene packaging.
- Add cleaning instructions.
- Avoid treatment or body-result claims.

Lane 4:
- Check all relevant Lane 1 items.
- Request safety documents.
- Check plug, voltage, battery, charging, manual, warning label, heat, and overheating risk.
- Do not approve direct sale until safety evidence is reviewed.

## Supplier Link Collection Fields

For each sample, collect:
- Supplier route.
- Supplier product URL.
- Variant/color selected.
- Product cost.
- Sample shipping cost.
- Estimated delivery time to US.
- Estimated delivery time to UK.
- Country of origin.
- HS code if available.
- Supplier rating/review notes.
- Document availability.

Definition:
- HS code: an international product classification code used for customs.

## Pass/Fail Rules

Pass:
- Product matches listing.
- Quality supports the intended selling price.
- Delivery and tracking are acceptable.
- Supplier can answer basic questions.
- US and UK market checks can continue.
- Product can be photographed honestly.

Fail:
- Product differs from listing.
- Product feels too cheap for intended price.
- Supplier delivery is too slow or unclear.
- Product has smell, sharp edge, weak hinge, poor stitching, weak light/electrical issue, or misleading photos.
- Supplier cannot provide required information.
