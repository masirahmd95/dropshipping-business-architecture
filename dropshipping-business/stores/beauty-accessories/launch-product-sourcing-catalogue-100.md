# Launch Product Sourcing Catalogue - 100 Beauty Candidates

## Purpose

This catalogue turns the top 100 beauty product candidates into a pre-sample buying guide.

Business meaning:
- It helps decide which products deserve sample orders.
- It gives a first cost-price range for each product.
- It records what product image must be checked before ordering.
- It separates simple products from products that need compliance proof before sale.

Important:
- These are target supplier cost ranges, not locked supplier quotes.
- Final cost prices must be replaced with the exact cost from the chosen supplier listing before any order is placed.
- Actual supplier product images should not be copied into the public GitHub repository unless image-use permission is confirmed.

## Key Definitions

- Cost price: the price paid to the supplier for the product before adding your selling margin.
- Landed cost: the product cost plus shipping, payment fees, taxes, packaging, returns allowance, and other costs needed to deliver the product.
- Supplier listing: the exact product page from the supplier that shows price, images, shipping, variants, reviews, and stock.
- SKU: stock keeping unit; a unique product or variant code used to track an item.
- Variant: a product option such as color, size, bundle size, or plug type.
- Reference image: an image used to understand the product type, not yet approved as a final store image.
- Supplier image: an image from the exact supplier listing being considered.

## Image Rule

For the public portfolio repo, this file uses image search links rather than copying seller images.

Reason:
- Seller and marketplace images are usually copyrighted.
- A product image must match the exact supplier SKU before we rely on it.
- Using a generic image can cause sample-order mistakes.

Operational rule:
- Before ordering a sample, capture the exact supplier listing URL and save the supplier image status as `Matched to supplier listing`.

## Pricing Assumptions

Currency:
- USD.

Cost range meaning:
- Low end: possible low-cost supplier price for a simple generic item.
- High end: safer planning number for a higher-quality listing, bundle, or faster route.

Excluded from these estimates:
- Final shipping.
- Import duty.
- Sales tax/VAT.
- Payment processing fees.
- Returns allowance.
- Custom packaging.

## Sample Ordering Guidance

Do not order all 100 samples at once.

Recommended order:
1. Sample the existing top 30 first.
2. Reject products with poor material, misleading images, unsafe packaging, or weak supplier communication.
3. Promote only the passed products into live product files.
4. Keep Lane 3 cosmetics and Lane 4 electrical products in preparation unless supplier documents are strong.

Definition:
- Supplier communication: how quickly and clearly the supplier responds to questions about price, stock, delivery, documents, and defects.

## Required Supplier Fields Before Sample Order

For every sample you consider ordering, capture:
- Supplier name.
- Supplier listing URL.
- Exact variant selected.
- Product cost.
- Shipping cost to US.
- Shipping cost to UK.
- Estimated delivery days to US.
- Estimated delivery days to UK.
- Supplier product image match status.
- Review count and rating if available.
- Return or defect policy.
- Compliance documents requested where needed.

## Verification Checklist

Operational verification:
- Every product has a plain-English description.
- Every product has a target supplier cost range.
- Every product has an image reference link.
- Products that touch skin, eyes, lips, or hair/scalp are not treated as risk-free.
- Cosmetics and electrical products are not approved for direct launch without documents.

Backend verification:
- Product names match the top-100 scorecard.
- Cost ranges are recorded as ranges, not false fixed prices.
- The catalogue separates estimated cost from final supplier cost.
- Image status is separated from product approval.

Visual verification:
- Supplier image must match the actual product variant before sample order.
- Final product-page images must show size, material, color, packaging, and use case clearly.
- Generic or AI-generated images must not be used as proof that a supplier product is correct.

## Playbook Lesson

For any business, never move from product idea to supplier order without a sourcing catalogue.

Reusable rule:
- Product idea -> cost estimate -> image reference -> exact supplier listing -> sample order -> inspection -> approval.
