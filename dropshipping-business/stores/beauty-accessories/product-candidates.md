# Product Candidates: Beauty Accessories

## Purpose

This file tracks product ideas before they are approved for the beauty accessories store.

Definition:
- Product candidate: a product idea being researched before it becomes a live store product.

## Decision Status Definitions

- Candidate: worth researching.
- Test: ready for sample order or small market test.
- Approved: passed demand, supplier, margin, compliance, and visual checks.
- Blocked: should not be sold.

## Store-Specific Approval Rules

- Do not approve products that require medical or treatment claims.
- Do not approve cosmetics without ingredient, label, batch, supplier, and claim checks.
- Do not approve electrical beauty devices without safety proof and sample checks.
- Do not use before/after imagery unless claim risk is reviewed.
- Do not approve reusable tools without cleaning instructions.

Definition:
- Claim risk: the risk that product wording or images promise a result the business cannot legally or honestly support.
