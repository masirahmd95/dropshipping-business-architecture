# Top 100 Beauty Product Scorecard - June 2026

## Purpose

This scorecard ranks beauty product candidates for a zero-inventory dropshipping launch.

Definition:
- Zero-inventory dropshipping: the store does not buy and hold stock in advance; a supplier ships the product after the customer orders.

Important:
- These are business research decisions, not final product approvals.
- Direct-sale products still need sample testing.
- Cosmetics, electrical products, and high-compliance products need stronger proof before launch.

Reference date:
- Trend and compliance signals were checked on 2026-06-13.

## Score Key

Scores are out of 35.

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Direct-sale priority | Strongest first-launch candidate after sample check |
| Sample first | Worth testing physically before direct sale |
| Affiliate-first | Better to recommend through an authorized seller before direct sale |
| Prepare only | Build future compliance path, but do not launch yet |
| Exclude for now | Too risky or operationally weak for this launch |

Definitions:
- Viral demand: demand driven by sudden social-media attention.
- Steady demand: demand that exists consistently over time.
- Evergreen demand: demand likely to remain useful across seasons.

## Excluded Or High-Compliance Preparation List

These products can be researched later, but should not be direct-sale launch products without expert review:

| Product | Reason |
|---|---|
| SPF/sunscreen | Often regulated as a drug in the United States and high claim risk |
| Acne treatment products | Treatment claims can move the product into drug/device territory |
| Hair-growth products | High claim risk and proof burden |
| Dandruff treatment products | Treatment category |
| LED therapy masks | Device/radiation and treatment-claim risk |
| Microcurrent facial devices | Device and safety risk |
| IPL hair removal devices | Radiation/electrical and safety risk |
| High-frequency acne wands | Device/treatment risk |
| Gel/acrylic nail chemical kits | Chemical safety and label risk |
| UV gel nail lamps bundled with chemical systems | Electrical, radiation, and chemical risk |
| Teeth whitening gels/devices | Dental/chemical/device risk |

## Verification Checklist

Operational verification:
- Top 30 direct-sale/sample-first products are selected.
- Lane 3, Lane 4, and Lane 5 products are not treated as simple accessories.
- Supplier route is matched by product risk.
- First launch basket avoids direct-sale high-compliance products.

Backend verification:
- Every product has category, demand type, compliance lane, and decision.
- Product table uses consistent decision labels.
- Launch decisions can later map into Shopify product tags or metafields.

Visual verification:
- Products chosen for launch have clear product-page image requirements.
- Products chosen for social content can be demonstrated honestly.
- Product pages will show sizing, material, use, cleaning, and warnings where relevant.
