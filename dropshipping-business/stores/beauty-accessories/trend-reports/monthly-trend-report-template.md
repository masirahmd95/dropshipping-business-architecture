## Report Month

```text
YYYY-MM
```

## Purpose

Use this report to decide which beauty products should be sampled, watched, rejected, prepared for compliance, or promoted in newsletters.

Definition:
- Product opportunity: a product idea backed by trend, customer, supplier, margin, and compliance evidence.

## Source Checklist

Record which sources were checked:

- Google Trends.
- Google Trending Now.
- TikTok/social trend observations.
- Amazon bestseller/review checks.
- Shopify trend reports.
- Supplier availability and cost checks.
- Store search terms.
- Email clicks.
- Product page views.
- Add-to-cart rate.
- Support questions.
- Refund/return reasons.

Definition:
- Add-to-cart rate: the percentage of product-page visitors who add the product to cart.

## Executive Summary

Plain-English summary:

```text
What changed this month?
What products look stronger?
What products look weaker?
What should we sample next?
What should we avoid?
```

## Verification Checklist

Operational verification:
- Report has a month and source list.
- Each sample recommendation has a reason.
- High-compliance products are not treated as direct-sale without review.

Backend verification:
- Product opportunities connect to product candidates.
- Scores use the monthly trend engine rules.
- Report is archived in the trend report folder.

Visual verification:
- Dashboard-ready metrics are clear.
- Charts avoid fake precision.
- Warnings are visible for compliance, margin, or supplier risk.
