# Beauty Lead Magnet And Newsletter Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines how the beauty store will attract leads, convert them into customers, and keep them returning.

Definitions:
- Lead magnet: a free useful asset offered in exchange for a sign-up.
- Newsletter: a recurring email sent to subscribers.

## Lead Magnet Library

| Asset | Working Title | Audience | Products It Supports | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PDF | 7-Day Beauty Organization Reset | Beauty storage and routine shoppers | organizers, brush cases, toiletry bags | Planned |
| PDF | Travel Beauty Packing Checklist | Travel shoppers | travel organizers, brush covers, cosmetic bags | Planned |
| PDF | Heatless Hair Routine Cheat Sheet | Hair accessory shoppers | heatless curl set, satin accessories, claw clips | Planned |
| PDF | Brush Cleaning And Replacement Calendar | Makeup tool shoppers | brush cleaning mat, reusable pads, brush cases | Planned |
| Quiz | Find Your Beauty Routine Type | Higher-intent leads | all category recommendations | Later |

Definition:
- Higher-intent lead: someone who gives stronger buying signals, such as completing a quiz or requesting a product alert.

## First Lead Magnet To Create

Start with:

```text
7-Day Beauty Organization Reset
```

Reason:
- It connects to low-risk launch products.
- It avoids medical claims.
- It supports organizers, travel bags, brush cases, and storage products.
- It gives the store a reason to email before product pages are fully live.

## Weekly Newsletter Structure

Use a simple weekly format:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| One beauty routine tip | Useful value without hard selling |
| One featured product | Drive purchase interest |
| One bundle or offer | Raise order value |
| One trend note | Make the store feel current |
| One clear call to action | Send reader to collection, product, or lead magnet |

Definition:
- Call to action: the button or link asking the reader to do something.

## Newsletter Calendar

## Offer Strategy

| Offer | Use Case | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome discount | First purchase conversion | One-use and margin checked |
| Bundle offer | Related accessories | Bundle margin checked |
| Free shipping threshold | Larger basket | US/UK shipping cost checked |
| Large order shipping discount | Higher cart value | Market-specific threshold |
| New arrival early access | Product launch excitement | Product must pass launch gates |

Definition:
- Guardrail: a rule that stops an offer from damaging margin or trust.

## Backend Records Required

- Lead magnet record.
- Form submission record.
- Consent record.
- Campaign record.
- Email event record.
- Incentive record.
- Segment record.
- Product interest record.

## Visual Verification

- Lead magnet cover looks professional.
- Signup form is not cluttered.
- Email preview works on mobile and desktop.
- Product images in newsletter match approved product photos.
- Offer terms are readable.
- Discount and shipping thresholds are not misleading.

## Reusable Lesson

The lead magnet should not be random content. It should attract the same person who is likely to buy the store's safest and most profitable first products.
