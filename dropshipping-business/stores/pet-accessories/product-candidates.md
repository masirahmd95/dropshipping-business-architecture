# Product Candidates: Pet Accessories

## Purpose

This file tracks product ideas before they are approved for the pet accessories store.

Definition:
- Product candidate: a product idea being researched before it becomes a live store product.

## Decision Status Definitions

- Candidate: worth researching.
- Test: ready for sample order or small market test.
- Approved: passed demand, supplier, margin, compliance, and visual checks.
- Blocked: should not be sold.

## Store-Specific Approval Rules

- Do not approve ingestible products at launch.
- Do not approve products with small detachable parts.
- Do not approve products making medical, calming, or guaranteed behavior claims.
- Do not approve without pet type, size range, and supervision guidance.

Definition:
- Guaranteed behavior claim: a promise that a product will make a pet behave in a specific way, such as stopping barking or anxiety.
