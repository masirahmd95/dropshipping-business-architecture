# Store Tracks

## Purpose

This folder contains the three store tracks being researched and set up from the shared dropshipping backend architecture.

Definition:
- Store track: a separate store idea with its own product candidates, suppliers, risks, and future brand.

## Current Store Tracks

| Store ID | Store Type | Status | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home_kitchen_utility` | Home/kitchen organization and utility accessories | Research | `home-kitchen-utility/store-profile.md` |
| `beauty_accessories` | Beauty tools and skincare-adjacent accessories | Research | `beauty-accessories/store-profile.md` |
| `pet_accessories` | Pet accessories and grooming tools | Research | `pet-accessories/store-profile.md` |

Each store also has a product candidate log:

- `home-kitchen-utility/product-candidates.md`
- `beauty-accessories/product-candidates.md`
- `pet-accessories/product-candidates.md`

## Shared Backend Rule

These stores share the same backend architecture, data model, integration categories, verification standards, and playbook method.

They do not share product catalogues, compliance rules, product claims, visual checks, or final branding.

Definition:
- Product claim: a statement about what a product does, such as "reduces shedding" or "improves skin."

## Setup Sequence

1. Create store profile.
2. Define product lanes.
3. Define blocked product types.
4. Define store-specific compliance rules.
5. Create product candidate scorecards.
6. Find suppliers.
7. Order samples.
8. Approve products.
9. Build storefront and customer experience.
10. Brand last.
