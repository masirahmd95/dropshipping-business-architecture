# Beauty Backend Control Room

## Purpose

This folder contains an offline staff-facing dashboard for the beauty dropshipping backend.

Definition:
- Staff-facing dashboard: a screen used by the business owner or operator, not the customer.

Business meaning:
- This is not branding and not the customer storefront.
- It is a local control room for launch readiness, sample ordering, CRM, marketing, trend intelligence, and missing work.

## How To Open

On Windows, double-click:

```text
open-control-room.cmd
```

Or open:

```text
index.html
```

No internet connection is required.

## What It Shows

- Launch readiness.
- Backend architecture modules.
- Top 30 sample order pack.
- US and UK market status.
- Product compliance lane mix.
- CRM and lead conversion setup.
- Monthly trend intelligence setup.
- Missing work before launch.

## Verification Notes

- This is a static offline view.
- It does not connect to Shopify, supplier apps, customer data, or payment accounts.
- Real customer data and credentials must never be placed in this folder.
