# Product File Template

## Purpose

Use this template for every product before it is approved for sale.

Definition:
- Product file: the proof folder for one product, showing what it is, who supplies it, what evidence exists, what claims are allowed, and what checks have passed.

## Verification Checklist

Operational verification:
- Supplier route is recorded.
- Sample status is recorded.
- Launch decision is recorded.
- Return and support notes are recorded.

Backend verification:
- SKU, store ID, supplier, compliance lane, decision status, and evidence status are complete.
- Claims are separated into approved and blocked.
- Missing evidence is visible.

Visual verification:
- Product images match the sample.
- Product page copy uses only approved claims.
- Warnings and instructions are visible.
- Mobile product page can show all key information clearly.
