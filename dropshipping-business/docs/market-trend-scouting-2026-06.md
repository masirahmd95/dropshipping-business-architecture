# Market Trend Scouting Report - June 2026

## Purpose

This report compares public demand signals from Amazon, AliExpress, TikTok Shop, Temu, Shopify merchant trends, Google Trends, and Pinterest-style search behavior before choosing the dropshipping niche.

Definition:
- Market trend scouting: the process of checking what customers appear to be buying, searching for, watching, reviewing, or discussing before choosing products.

Important limitation:
- We cannot access private marketplace databases unless the business owns seller accounts or paid research tools. Amazon, AliExpress, TikTok Shop, and Temu do not expose a clean public table of exact unit sales by niche.
- We can access public and semi-public demand signals: bestseller pages, review counts, visible order counts where available, search trend data, social commerce reports, supplier availability, marketplace news, and category growth reports.

Definition:
- Demand signal: evidence that buyers may want a product. Examples include high search interest, high marketplace ranking, fast review growth, viral videos, frequent supplier listings, or repeated appearances in bestseller lists.

## Definitions

- GMV: gross merchandise value; the total value of goods sold through a marketplace before deducting refunds, fees, or costs.
- BSR: best seller rank; Amazon's category ranking signal for how well a product is selling compared with other products in that category.
- Review velocity: how quickly a product gains new reviews over time. It can indicate sales momentum, but it is not the same as exact sales.
- Sell-through: how quickly available inventory sells.
- Niche: a focused market segment, such as "home kitchen organizers" rather than "home products."
- AOV: average order value; the average amount a customer spends per order.
- CAC: customer acquisition cost; the cost of getting one paying customer.
- Margin: the money left after product cost, shipping, payment fees, marketplace fees, returns, and advertising.
- SKU: stock keeping unit; one unique product or variant code.
- Impulse product: a product customers buy quickly because the benefit is easy to understand.
- Product compliance: checking that a product is legal, safe, allowed by platforms, allowed by payment providers, and honestly advertised.
- Supplier depth: how many credible suppliers can provide similar products with acceptable quality and delivery.
- Price floor: the lowest market price customers can easily find. Temu and AliExpress are useful for checking this.

## Important Marketplace Findings

### TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop is no longer just a side experiment. WIRED reported that analytics firm EchoTik estimated TikTok Shop sold about $19 billion globally from July through September 2025, with the United States accounting for about $4 billion to $4.5 billion. Business Insider also reported that TikTok Shop was the No. 3 fastest-growing brand in the United States in 2025 by purchase consideration, and that it drove more than $500 million in United States sales over Black Friday through Cyber Monday.

Business meaning:
- TikTok should be treated as both a marketing channel and a product research engine.
- Products should be selected partly by whether they can be demonstrated clearly in short videos.

Definition:
- Social commerce: selling products inside or directly through social media platforms.

### Temu

Temu is a useful signal for mass-market demand and price pressure. PDD Holdings, Temu's parent, reported 2025 revenue of RMB431.8 billion, or about US$61.8 billion, across its platforms. Temu has expanded internationally and is designed around low prices and broad assortment.

Business meaning:
- Temu tells us which products customers expect to be cheap.
- If a product is easy to find on Temu at a very low price, our store needs a better angle: better quality proof, faster shipping, bundle value, specialist curation, content, warranty, or brand trust.
- Temu should not be treated as a clean supplier shortcut. The platform has faced product safety and illegal-goods scrutiny, including a 2026 EU fine reported by The Guardian. That makes product compliance and supplier verification essential.

Definition:
- Price pressure: the downward force on selling price when many competitors sell similar goods cheaply.

### Amazon

Amazon remains the strongest public benchmark for mature demand. June 2026 commerce coverage highlighted kitchen reset products, reusable household items, cleaning and home tools, summer fashion, hydration products, beauty, K-beauty, and practical tech accessories.

Business meaning:
- Amazon is useful for proving that customers already buy a category.
- It is not easy to dropship directly against Amazon unless the product is differentiated, bundled, faster, better explained, or marketed through a sharper audience.

### AliExpress

AliExpress remains useful for supplier discovery and cross-border product research. It is made up of independent sellers offering products internationally, and current public summaries note global logistics improvements such as faster delivery programs.

Business meaning:
- AliExpress is useful for finding potential products and suppliers.
- It should not be trusted blindly. Every candidate supplier needs sample ordering, delivery checks, quality checks, customer review checks, and compliance checks.

## Recommended Direction

The strongest early direction is:

```text
Everyday problem-solving accessories for home, beauty, pet, and portable lifestyle use.
```

This does not mean a messy general store. It means a disciplined product research lane:
- Small products.
- Clear visual benefit.
- Low product safety risk.
- Low intellectual property risk.
- Suitable for bundles.
- Suitable for short-form video.
- Not easily destroyed by Temu price comparison.

Best first sub-niche options:

1. Home and kitchen organization:
   - Drawer organizers.
   - Food storage helpers.
   - Reusable cleaning accessories.
   - Countertop organization.
   - Small prep tools.

2. Beauty and personal-care accessories:
   - Makeup organizers.
   - Skincare storage.
   - Lash/brow tools.
   - Hair/scalp tools that do not make medical claims.
   - Travel toiletry systems.

3. Pet accessories:
   - Grooming tools.
   - Travel bowls.
   - Leash and storage accessories.
   - Wash and cleanup helpers.
   - Non-consumable enrichment products.

## Categories To Avoid First

Avoid these as the first niche even if demand is high:

- Supplements.
- Ingestibles.
- Children's toys.
- Baby products.
- Electrical chargers.
- Batteries.
- Medical devices.
- Products making health, weight loss, hair growth, or treatment claims.
- Counterfeit or unauthorized branded goods.
- Team, league, celebrity, or event merchandise without proof of authorization.

Definition:
- Health claim: a statement that a product treats, prevents, cures, improves, or diagnoses a health condition. These claims can trigger legal and advertising restrictions.

## Verification Standard For Product Research

Operational verification:
- At least three independent demand signals are recorded.
- At least two supplier options are identified where possible.
- Sample order is required before launch unless using an already vetted supplier.
- Shipping time, tracking quality, packaging, and product quality are checked.

Backend tidiness verification:
- Product candidate record is complete.
- Product has one clear niche cluster.
- Product compliance review exists before publication.
- Margin calculation includes fees, shipping, returns allowance, and ad allowance.
- Supplier record includes contact, shipping rules, return rules, and evidence links.

Visual verification:
- Product photos are not misleading.
- Variant images match the selected variant.
- Product page clearly shows size, use, contents, and limitations.
- Video demo, if used, shows the actual product function.

Definition:
- Variant: a version of a product, such as a different color, size, pack quantity, or material.
