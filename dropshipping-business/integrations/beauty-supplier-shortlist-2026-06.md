# Beauty Supplier Shortlist - June 2026

## Purpose

This shortlist identifies supplier and sourcing routes for the beauty dropshipping store.

Definition:
- Supplier route: the way the business gets products fulfilled, such as a dropshipping app, direct supplier, brand partnership, or affiliate program.

Important:
- Public app ratings and platform claims are useful first checks, not final proof.
- Final approval still requires samples, product files, supplier documents, and trial orders.

Reference date:
- Public app and pricing signals were checked on 2026-06-13.

- MOQ: minimum order quantity; the smallest number of units a supplier will sell in one order.

## Supplier Rules By Product Risk

### Simple Accessories

Use:
- DSers.
- CJdropshipping.
- EPROLO.
- AppScenic.
- Syncee.

Examples:
- Hair clips.
- Headbands.
- Organizers.
- Toiletry bags.
- Makeup brush cases.

Required checks:
- Sample order.
- Delivery time.
- Product photos vs real sample.
- Material quality.
- Packaging quality.
- Customer review scan.

### Skin-Contact Tools

Use:
- CJdropshipping.
- Syncee.
- Direct sample sourcing through Alibaba or Global Sources.

Examples:
- Face brush.
- Gua sha.
- Scalp brush.
- Powder puff.
- Reusable pads.

Required checks:
- Material information.
- Hygiene packaging.
- Cleaning instructions.
- Claim review.
- Sample inspection.

### Cosmetics

Use:
- Shopify Collective.
- Authorized brand partners.
- TikTok Shop affiliate.
- Amazon affiliate or beauty affiliate networks.

Examples:
- Lip oil.
- Blush.
- Mascara.
- Toner pads.
- Fragrance mist.

Required checks:
- Ingredient list.
- Label and packaging proof.
- Batch code process.
- Responsible party.
- Safety substantiation.
- Authorized resale proof.

### Electrical Beauty Products

Use:
- Authorized supplier.
- Brand-direct route.
- Supplier with documented safety certification.

Examples:
- LED vanity mirror.
- Heated eyelash curler.
- Hair dryer.
- Skincare mini fridge.

Required checks:
- Safety certificates.
- Plug and voltage check.
- Battery/shipping check.
- Manual and warning check.
- Sample operation check.

### High-Compliance Products

Use:
- Affiliate-first.
- Authorized brand partnership.
- Expert-reviewed direct supply only later.

Examples:
- SPF.
- Acne treatment.
- Hair growth.
- LED therapy masks.
- IPL devices.
- Teeth whitening.

Required checks:
- Legal classification.
- Regulatory proof.
- Authorized supplier proof.
- Claims review.
- Payment and platform rule check.

## Cheapest Deployable Scalable Stack

Recommended starting stack:

| Function | Tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Commerce backend | Shopify Basic | Cheapest serious ecommerce base with products, checkout, payments, orders, analytics, apps, and scale path |
| Supplier app for simple accessories | DSers free/basic or CJdropshipping free-to-install | Lowest practical cost for testing simple goods |
| Supplier backup | EPROLO or AppScenic | Adds branding or structured supplier route if first supplier fails |
| Authorized cosmetics route | Shopify Collective where eligible | Better for brand-authorized no-inventory resale |
| Affiliate-first high-risk route | TikTok Shop affiliate / Amazon Associates | Lets us test demand without direct compliance responsibility |
| Product validation | Markdown docs now, Airtable/Google Sheets later | Avoid software cost until validation workflow proves itself |
| Analytics | Shopify analytics, GA4, Google Tag Manager, TikTok Pixel, Meta Pixel | Basic measurement from launch |
| Email | Shopify Messaging/Email first | Low-cost early lifecycle messaging |
| Support | Shopify Inbox first | Low-cost customer support starting point |
| Accounting | Shopify exports first, QuickBooks/Xero later | Avoid monthly accounting apps until sales volume requires it |

Definition:
- Deployable: practical to set up and run, not just theoretically good.

Upgrade triggers:
- Move from Shopify Basic to Grow when team/staff needs increase or transaction economics justify it.
- Add Klaviyo when email revenue matters enough to justify the monthly cost.
- Add Gorgias/Zendesk when support volume is too high for Shopify Inbox.
- Add A2X plus QuickBooks/Xero when payouts, fees, refunds, taxes, and marketplace sales become hard to reconcile manually.
- Add custom dashboard when Shopify reports cannot answer margin, supplier, compliance, and channel questions clearly.

## Supplier Verification Checklist

Before approving a supplier:
- Public rating or review signal checked.
- Recent negative reviews scanned.
- Supplier support channel tested.
- Sample ordered.
- Delivery time recorded.
- Tracking quality checked.
- Product quality checked.
- Packaging checked.
- Return/defect process checked.
- Compliance documents requested.
- Product photo usage permission checked.
- Branded product authorization checked if applicable.

## Backend Meaning

The supplier shortlist becomes supplier master data.

Definition:
- Supplier master data: the official supplier record used by the business.

Required supplier record fields:
- Supplier name.
- Supplier route type.
- Product categories supported.
- Countries shipped from.
- Countries shipped to.
- Typical delivery time.
- Cost model.
- Public rating signal.
- Sample status.
- Document status.
- Compliance risk.
- Support contact.
- Backup supplier.
- Approval status.

## Verification Checklist

Operational verification:
- At least one primary and one backup route exist for simple accessories.
- Cosmetics and high-compliance products are not sourced through blind low-cost suppliers.
- Supplier decision is matched to product risk.
- No supplier is approved without a sample check.

Backend verification:
- Supplier names and route types are consistent.
- Supplier records separate public rating, sample proof, and document proof.
- Backup supplier field exists for launch products.
- Supplier approval status is not confused with product approval status.

Visual verification:
- Supplier-provided product images are checked against samples.
- Any image used on product pages accurately represents size, material, color, and packaging.
- Product pages do not use supplier claims until claims are reviewed.
