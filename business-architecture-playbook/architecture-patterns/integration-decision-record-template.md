# Integration Decision Record Template

## Purpose

Use this template to make integration decisions deliberately.

Definition:
- Integration decision record: a short document explaining why one external tool or service was chosen over alternatives.

## Template

```text
# Integration Decision: [Area]

## Business Need

What business job must this integration perform?

## Key Definitions

Define any terms needed to understand the decision.

## Options Considered

List candidate providers or approaches.

## Decision

Chosen provider or approach:

## Reason

Why this choice fits the business.

## Data Movement

What data moves in and out?

## Business Rules

What rules must the integration follow?

## Risks

What could go wrong?

## Fallback

What is the backup option?

## Verification Checklist

How will we prove it works operationally, stays tidy in the backend, and looks right where visual surfaces exist?

## Playbook Lesson

What can be reused for future businesses?
```

Definitions:
- Data movement: how information passes between systems.
- Business rule: a rule that tells the system what should happen.
- Visual surface: anything a customer or staff member sees, such as a dashboard, email, receipt, checkout page, or admin screen.
