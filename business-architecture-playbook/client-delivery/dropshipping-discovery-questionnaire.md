# Dropshipping Discovery Questionnaire

## Purpose

Use this questionnaire before building a dropshipping business system.

Definition:
- Discovery: the process of understanding the business before designing the system.

## Business Model

- What product category do you want to sell?
- Who is the target customer?
- What problem, desire, or occasion causes the customer to buy?
- What countries will you sell to first?
- Are you aiming for low-ticket, mid-ticket, or premium products?

Definition:
- Target customer: the type of person most likely to buy.

## Suppliers

- Which suppliers will you use?
- Do suppliers provide an API, app, CSV feed, or manual product data?
- Where are supplier warehouses located?
- What are supplier processing times?
- What shipping countries do suppliers support?
- What happens when a supplier is out of stock?
- Who handles damaged, delayed, or missing items?

Definition:
- CSV feed: a spreadsheet-like file used to transfer product or inventory data.

## Products

- How many products will launch in the first version?
- Will products have variants such as size or colour?
- Who approves product titles, descriptions, images, and prices?
- What margin must each product achieve?
- Are bundles required?

Definition:
- Bundle: a product offer made from multiple items sold together.

## Payments, Tax, And Fraud

- What country is the business registered in?
- Which payment provider accounts are available?
- Which currencies must be accepted?
- Which countries create tax obligations?
- What risk level should trigger manual fraud review?

Definition:
- Manual fraud review: a human checks an order before fulfilment.

## Operations

- Who will handle customer support?
- What support channels are required: email, chat, phone, social?
- What is the return policy?
- What is the refund policy?
- What order volume should the system handle in the first year?
- What alerts should staff receive?

Definition:
- Order volume: the number of orders the business expects to process.

## Marketing And Reporting

- Which marketing channels will be used?
- Which ad platforms will be used?
- Is email marketing required at launch?
- Is SMS marketing required at launch?
- What reports should the owner see weekly?
- Which accounting software will be used?

Definition:
- Marketing channel: a source of customer attention, such as Google, TikTok, Meta, email, or influencers.

## Required User Inputs

Before implementation, the user should provide:

- Chosen ecommerce platform
- Supplier list
- Target countries
- Payment provider
- Tax approach or accountant guidance
- Email/SMS platform preference
- Support platform preference
- Accounting platform
- Analytics and advertising channels

Definition:
- Accountant guidance: advice from a finance professional about tax, bookkeeping, and reporting duties.
