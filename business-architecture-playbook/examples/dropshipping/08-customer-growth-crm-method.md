# Customer Growth And CRM Method

## Purpose

This method explains how to design CRM, lead capture, email marketing, incentives, and trend intelligence for a dropshipping business.

Definition:
- CRM: customer relationship management; the system for storing leads, customers, consent, purchases, preferences, and marketing status.

## Core Lesson

Marketing should be built as a data system.

It should answer:
- Who joined?
- Why did they join?
- What permission did they give?
- What did they click?
- What did they buy?
- What offer did they use?
- What products are gaining demand?
- What should be sampled next?

## Step 1: Define The Customer Database

Minimum records:
- Lead.
- Customer.
- Consent.
- Form submission.
- Segment.
- Campaign.
- Email event.
- Incentive.
- Product interest.

Definition:
- Segment: a rule-based group of people, such as UK leads interested in travel beauty products.

## Step 2: Create Value-Led Lead Capture

Do not ask people to "join our newsletter" without a reason.

Better:
- Free checklist.
- Free guide.
- Quiz result.
- Product launch alert.
- Early access.

Definition:
- Lead magnet: a useful free asset offered in exchange for a sign-up.

## Step 3: Store Consent Correctly

Every marketing system needs:
- Consent status.
- Consent source.
- Consent date.
- Privacy notice version.
- Unsubscribe date if withdrawn.

Definition:
- Consent source: where permission was given, such as footer form, popup, quiz, checkout, or lead magnet page.

## Step 4: Build Lifecycle Flows

Required flows:
- Welcome and lead magnet delivery.
- First-purchase incentive.
- Weekly product newsletter.
- New arrival alerts.
- Abandoned cart.
- Post-purchase education.
- Review request.
- Cross-sell and bundle.
- Winback.

Definition:
- Lifecycle flow: a sequence of messages based on where the person is in the buying journey.

## Step 5: Control Incentives

Incentives should increase conversion without destroying margin.

Useful incentives:
- First purchase discount.
- Free shipping threshold.
- Bundle discount.
- Early access.
- Large order shipping discount.

Rule:
- Every incentive needs a market, value, expiry, margin gate, and visual review.

Definition:
- Margin gate: a check proving the offer still leaves enough profit.

## Step 6: Create A Monthly Trend Engine

The product research system should run every month.

Signals:
- Public search trends.
- Social trends.
- Marketplace demand.
- Supplier availability.
- Product costs.
- Store search terms.
- Email clicks.
- Product views.
- Add-to-cart rate.
- Customer questions.
- Return reasons.

## Step 7: Make The Dashboard Simple

The backend can be analytical, but the front end should be easy to read.

Dashboard views:
- Lead funnel.
- Email performance.
- US vs UK split.
- Offer performance.
- Product trend scores.
- Product opportunity map.
- Category share.
- Backend alerts.

Definition:
- Category share: the percentage of the store's own leads, orders, or revenue connected to a product category. It is not global market share unless a proper external market dataset is added.


## Reusable Lesson

CRM is the business memory of customer demand. When it is connected to product research, marketing stops being random promotion and becomes a feedback system for choosing what to sell next.
