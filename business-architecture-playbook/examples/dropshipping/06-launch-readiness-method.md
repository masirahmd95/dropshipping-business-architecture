# Launch Readiness Method

## Purpose

This method explains how to move from product research to a controlled ecommerce launch.

Definition:
- Launch readiness: proving that products, suppliers, systems, customer support, compliance, and visual presentation are ready before customers can buy.

## Core Principle

Do not launch because a product looks exciting.

Launch when the business can prove:
- What the product is.
- Who supplies it.
- Whether it can be sold safely.
- How much margin it should make.
- What claims are allowed.
- How customers will receive tracking, support, returns, and refunds.
- Whether the product page looks accurate.

## Step 1: Pick A Controlled Launch Market

Start with one main country.

Definition:
- Launch market: the country or region where the store first sells.

Why:
- Tax, shipping, returns, compliance, delivery time, and customer expectations vary by country.
- "Worldwide shipping" should come later, after the operating model works.

## Step 2: Create Launch Gates

Every product must pass required gates:
- Product decision.
- Supplier review.
- Product file.
- Pricing.
- Claims.
- Visual review.
- Operations review.

Definition:
- Gate: a required checkpoint that stops work from moving forward until proof exists.

## Step 3: Build A Sample Plan

Start with a controlled sample batch.

Definition:
- Sample batch: a group of products ordered for inspection before customer launch.

The sample plan should define:
- Product.
- Supplier route.
- Backup supplier route.
- Product file ID.
- Compliance lane.
- Inspection checks.
- Pass/fail rules.

## Step 4: Choose The Cheapest Serious Stack

Choose tools that cover core operations without unnecessary cost.

Definition:
- Backend stack: the group of tools running the hidden business operations.

For dropshipping, the starting stack should cover:
- Commerce backend.
- Supplier import and fulfilment.
- Payments.
- Tax.
- Fraud.
- Analytics.
- Marketing events.
- Email.
- Support.
- Accounting export.

## Step 5: Convert Decisions Into Records

The launch is not ready until decisions become records.

Records needed:
- Product candidate.
- Market signal.
- Product file.
- Supplier route.
- Sample test.
- Pricing review.
- Product claim.
- Visual verification.
- Launch decision.

Definition:
- Record: stored information about one business object or decision.

## Step 6: Verify Three Ways

Operational verification:
- The business process works.

Backend verification:
- The hidden records and rules are clean.

Visual verification:
- Customer and staff screens look accurate and professional.

## Codex Prompt

```text
Create a launch readiness method for this ecommerce business. Define all terms in plain English. Include launch market, launch gates, sample plan, cheapest serious backend stack, required records, and operational/backend/visual verification.
```

## Reusable Lesson

Launch readiness is the bridge between research and sales.

It turns a product idea into a controlled business asset with proof, rules, supplier logic, and verification.
