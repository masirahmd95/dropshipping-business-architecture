# Market Trend Research Method For Dropshipping

## Purpose

This method explains how to choose a dropshipping niche using evidence instead of guessing.

Definition:
- Evidence-based niche selection: choosing a product category because multiple independent signals suggest demand, margin potential, supplier availability, and manageable risk.

## Core Rule

Do not ask only:

```text
What is trending?
```

Ask:

```text
What is trending, legal to sell, operationally manageable, profitable after fees, and capable of being differentiated?
```

Definition:
- Differentiation: the reason a customer would buy from this store instead of a cheaper or larger competitor.

## Step 1: Collect Demand Signals

Use multiple platforms:

- Amazon: bestseller rank, reviews, customer-loved pages, search suggestions, price ranges.
- TikTok Shop: trending products, creator content, product demonstrations, affiliate activity.
- Temu: price floor, product saturation, low-cost competitor pressure.
- AliExpress: supplier depth, order counts where visible, reviews, delivery options.
- Google Trends: search interest over time and geography.
- Pinterest: visual/lifestyle trend direction.
- Shopify trend reports: categories working for independent merchants.

Definition:
- Product saturation: when many sellers offer near-identical products, usually pushing prices and margins down.

## Step 2: Classify Each Product Idea

Use one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate | Interesting but not proven | Research more |
| Watchlist | Good trend, but not ready | Recheck later |
| Test | Good enough for sample order and landing page test | Prepare test |
| Approved | Passed demand, supplier, margin, and compliance checks | Import to catalogue |
| Blocked | Too risky, illegal, unsafe, or unprofitable | Do not sell |

Definition:
- Landing page test: a simple product page or campaign used to test customer interest before committing deeply.

## Step 3: Score The Niche

Score from 1 to 5:

- Demand strength.
- Supplier availability.
- Shipping simplicity.
- Gross margin potential.
- Compliance risk.
- Return risk.
- Content potential.
- Differentiation potential.

Definition:
- Gross margin: selling price minus direct cost of the product and fulfilment before broader business expenses.

## Step 4: Apply The Compliance Gate

Block or delay:

- Counterfeit goods.
- Unauthorized branded goods.
- Products using protected logos or designs without permission.
- Supplements and ingestibles without proper compliance support.
- Children's toys without safety documentation.
- Baby products without safety documentation.
- Electrical products without certification evidence.
- Products making medical or health claims.

Definition:
- Certification evidence: documents proving that a product meets required safety or regulatory standards.

## Step 5: Check The Price Floor

Use Temu and AliExpress to estimate how cheaply customers can find similar goods.

If the price floor is too low, the product needs one or more of:

- Better quality proof.
- Faster shipping.
- Better bundle.
- Better instructions.
- Better customer support.
- Better niche positioning.
- Better product photography and video.

Definition:
- Price floor: the lowest visible price that shapes what customers think a product should cost.

## Step 6: Require A Sample Order

Before launch, order the product and check:

- Delivery time.
- Packaging.
- Product quality.
- Variant accuracy.
- Size accuracy.
- Safety markings where relevant.
- Whether photos match the actual product.
- Whether the product can be demonstrated honestly.

Definition:
- Sample order: an order placed by the business to inspect a product before selling it to customers.

## Step 7: Translate Research Into Backend Records

Each product idea should become a structured record, not just a note.

Minimum fields:

- Product idea.
- Niche cluster.
- Customer problem.
- Demand signals.
- Supplier options.
- Price floor.
- Estimated cost.
- Target price.
- Margin estimate.
- Compliance risk.
- Sample status.
- Decision status.

Definition:
- Structured record: information stored in a consistent format so it can be filtered, compared, automated, and audited later.

## Codex Prompt

Use this prompt for future niche checks:

```text
Research this dropshipping niche using Amazon, AliExpress, TikTok Shop, Temu, Google Trends, and Shopify-style trend signals. Define all technical terms. Separate exact sales data from inferred demand signals. Score the niche for demand, supplier availability, shipping simplicity, margin, compliance risk, return risk, TikTok content potential, and differentiation. Recommend whether to approve, test, watch, or block it. Then update the business docs and playbook with the result.
```
