# Business Architecture Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook is created while building the dropshipping business.

Definition:
- Playbook: a reusable guide that explains a method step by step.

The rule is simple:

```text
Build the business.
Document the method.
Turn repeated decisions into reusable architecture.
```

## Current Case Study

The current case study is a dropshipping business.

The first focus is backend architecture:

- Product data
- Supplier management
- Inventory
- Pricing
- Payments
- Tax
- Fraud
- Orders
- Fulfilment
- Tracking
- Returns
- Support
- Marketing automation
- Analytics
- Accounting
- Verification

Definition:
- Case study: a real example used to learn and create reusable method.

## Method

For every business system:

1. Identify the business model.
2. Map the customer journey.
3. Map the staff and operations journey.
4. Identify backend functions.
5. Choose integrations.
6. Design the data model.
7. Define business rules.
8. Verify operational, backend, and visual quality.
9. Document reusable lessons.

Definition:
- Business rule: a rule that tells the system what should happen, such as "if payment fails, do not send the order to the supplier."
